About the program

A welcoming pathway for every young player.

Players are grouped according to age, experience and assessed playing ability. As their skills, attendance and commitment develop, they can progress to the next group.

  • Age-appropriate, ability-based coaching
  • Safe, enjoyable learning environment
  • Technical skills, movement and match understanding
  • Discipline, confidence and good sportsmanship

Training pathway

A program for every stage.

Each level combines tennis fundamentals with movement, games and personal development.

Ready for training

What players should bring.

Tennis racket, comfortable sports clothing, suitable trainers and a water bottle. A limited number of loan rackets may be available for beginners.

For parents

Simple preparation matters.

Players should arrive 10 minutes early. Parents should communicate absences, relevant medical information and collect younger children promptly after training.
